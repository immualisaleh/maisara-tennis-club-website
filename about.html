Who We Are

Maisara Tennis Club is a tennis development club in Zanzibar, supporting juniors, adults, and wheelchair tennis players.

Our Mission

To promote tennis excellence and community participation in Zanzibar.

Our Vision

To become a leading tennis development centre in East Africa.
